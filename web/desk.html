Check in an invited guest

For guests who can’t scan the QR. Search the guest list by name, number or the team member who invited them.

Add a walk-in

For a guest who isn’t on the list. If the number turns out to be on the guest list, they’re checked in as that guest. Leave “Who are they with?” empty to hand them to the next free team member.

Walk-ins today

Did a guest use a different number than the one on the list? Press Link to guest, pick them from the guest list, and they’re counted as that guest: the inviter gets the credit and an alert, and the new number is remembered.
